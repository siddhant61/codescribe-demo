A note on evidence

A demonstration, not a benchmark.

These artifacts are a historical snapshot of an analysis, not a live scan. Counts vary between the reports and graph export, so they should not be treated as a single verified measure of coverage or accuracy. The demo does not establish improvements in delivery performance or onboarding speed.

Use it to inspect the approach and its outputs, then verify any claim against the relevant source revision.
